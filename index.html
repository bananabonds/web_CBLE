Ranked by urgency, the same number the Adaptive Planner uses: Gap = your target minus a cautious estimate of how well you know the subject (first-time accuracy, older results fading, small samples penalised). Priority is the confidence-weighted score over every attempt (a correct guess counts less than a sure answer; a confident wrong answer counts against you more), shown alongside for context. Add questions by pasting 

        
Paste, check the preview, then press the button. Nothing is saved until you press it, and then it is saved immediately (no extra Save step).

        

          Accepted formats
          
Plain text (blank line or numbering between questions)
1. Which drug class ends in -pril?
A. Beta blockers
B. ACE inhibitors
C. Statins
D. Diuretics
Answer: B

Mark the right option with * instead of an Answer line:
A. Liver
B. Kidney *

Start a new bank inside one paste with a header line:
# Pharmacology

JSON: [{"text":"...","options":["a","b","c","d"],"correct":1}]
   or {"Bank name":[ ...questions... ]}
Python: Bank_Name = [ {"text":"...","options":[...],"correct":0}, ... ]
(correct is the position of the right option, counting from 0)

        

        
        

          
          
        

        

        
      


      

        
Questions in this bank  

        
Edits here are staged: nothing is saved until you press the Save / Commit button that appears at the bottom. Questions are never deleted or reordered (your history refers to them by position); Retire hides a question from all samples instead.

        

        

        
        

        

          

          

        

      


      

        
Bank problems 

        
Questions you tagged Other (or told to skip) in the Mistake Log. They stay out of samples until you fix them. Fix a Local or Repository question, then press Mark fixed to put it back. Built-in questions can't be edited, so make a corrected copy instead.
