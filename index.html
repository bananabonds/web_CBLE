Study Priority Ranking (most urgent first)

Ranked by urgency, the same number the Adaptive Planner uses: Gap = your target minus a cautious estimate of how well you know the subject (first-time accuracy, older results fading, small samples penalised). Priority is the confidence-weighted score over every attempt (a correct guess counts less than a sure answer; a confident wrong answer counts against you more), shown alongside for context. Unique / bank and Open come from your test history and Mistake Log.

Confidence Calibration

Does your confidence match your results? When you say “Confident”, are you right about 75% of the time? Calibration shows where your gut can be trusted and where it can't.

Score Forecast (probability of each possible score)

Each subject's true chance of getting a fresh question right is estimated from your first-seen answers (older results fade). That gives a probability for every possible number-correct on the real exam (a PMF), summed across subjects. The shaded chart below projects it forward to exam day. Everything here is a model with adjustable assumptions — open Settings & assumptions to change them.

Add questions by pasting 

        
Paste, check the preview, then press the button. Nothing is saved until you press it, and then it is saved immediately (no extra Save step).

        

          Accepted formats
          
Plain text (blank line or numbering between questions)
1. Which drug class ends in -pril?
A. Beta blockers
B. ACE inhibitors
C. Statins
D. Diuretics
Answer: B

Mark the right option with * instead of an Answer line:
A. Liver
B. Kidney *

Start a new bank inside one paste with a header line:
# Pharmacology

JSON: [{"text":"...","options":["a","b","c","d"],"correct":1}]
   or {"Bank name":[ ...questions... ]}
Python: Bank_Name = [ {"text":"...","options":[...],"correct":0}, ... ]
(correct is the position of the right option, counting from 0)

Built-in questions can't be edited, so make a corrected copy instead.
